Apple announced a major expansion of its on-device machine learning capabilities at WWDC 2026. The new Core AI framework replaces Core ML with a unified pipeline that allows developers to run large language models, image generators, and speech recognition entirely on the iPhone's Neural Engine and GPU. Benchmarks show up to 181 tokens per second on the iPhone 17 Pro for quantized 600-million-parameter models, a 1.6x improvement over the previous MLX runtime. The framework introduces pipelined GPU execution, which overlaps computation across the Neural Engine and GPU cores to hide latency. For the first time, Apple is also offering a built-in 3-billion-parameter language model that ships with the operating system, available to all apps through the FoundationModels framework at no additional cost. Developers can fine-tune this base model on their own datasets using Apple's new on-device training APIs. Privacy remains a core focus: all inference and training stays on-device by default, with no data leaving the phone. Industry analysts called it Apple's most significant AI infrastructure announcement since the Neural Engine debuted in 2017.
